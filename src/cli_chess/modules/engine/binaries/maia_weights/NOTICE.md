# Maia weight files

These neural network weight files were downloaded unmodified from:

https://github.com/CSSLab/maia-chess/releases/download/v1.0/maia-<ELO>.pb.gz

Source repository: https://github.com/CSSLab/maia-chess (tag `v1.0`)
Project site: https://maiachess.com

Maia is developed by the Computational Social Science Lab (CSSLab), University of
Toronto. The weights are distributed under the GNU General Public License, matching
cli-chess's own license.

Levels included: 1100, 1200, 1300, 1400, 1500, 1600, 1700, 1800, 1900.
